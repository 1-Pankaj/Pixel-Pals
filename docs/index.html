Meet the pals

Each one has a home, a voice and little ways of their own. Click them, drag them, feed them. They have opinions.

Here to keep you company, never in your way.

Never in your way

Pet your pal while you type, game or watch videos. Nothing you're doing gets interrupted, and clicks on the empty space around them go straight through.

Eyes that follow you

Wherever your cursor goes, they watch it. Even on a second screen.

Over 1,000 little messages

Morning hellos, tiny jokes, animal facts and kind notes. You'll hardly ever hear the same one twice.

Gentle reminders

Sip some water, stretch, rest your eyes, sit up tall. Soft nudges, never nagging.

Private and offline

No sign-ups, no accounts, no internet needed. Nothing ever leaves your computer.

Light as a feather

A tiny download that opens instantly and barely uses any power. Every pixel and sound is made on the spot.
